When feelings feel too big

If you might hurt yourself, someone else, or you do not feel safe, tell a trusted adult right now. In the U.S. or Canada, call or text 988. If danger is immediate, call emergency services.

Block By Block can help with coping skills, but it is not a crisis service and it does not replace a therapist, doctor, parent, guardian, counselor, or emergency support.

1. Find a person

Go near a parent, guardian, teacher, counselor, coach, relative, or another safe adult.

2. Say it clearly

Try: “I am not feeling safe and I need help right now.”

3. Make the space safer

Move away from anything you could use to hurt yourself and stay near another person.
