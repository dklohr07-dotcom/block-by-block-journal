👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Guide

Goal: Give youths a safe, playful place to name emotions, practice coping skills, and build confidence block by block.

How to use: Start with a mood check-in, one journal quest, one game, and one calming realm. Keep it light and pressure-free.

Privacy: This prototype saves entries locally in the browser. For a full product, add secure accounts, parent controls, and clear privacy policies.

Minecraft-inspired sayings: “Respawn point set,” “Craft calm,” “Mine one brave thought,” “Place the next block,” and “Your safe base is protected.”

Made to help young builders place one brave block at a time. 🧱
