COLD WATER RESET
Splash potion — instant calm activation
1. Run cold water over your wrists for 30 seconds
2. The cold activates your body's dive reflex — a built-in calm switch!
3. Take 3 slow breaths while you do it
4. Notice your heartbeat starting to slow. That's /effect @s calm working!
🏡
SAFE PLACE — SET YOUR SPAWN POINT
Your mental respawn point when things feel overwhelming
1. Close your eyes. Take 3 slow breaths. Type /spawn
2. Picture your safest, most peaceful place
3. What do you see? Hear? Smell? Feel there?
4. Stay 2–3 minutes. You can respawn here anytime you need.
💪
MUSCLE RELAXATION — TAKE OFF YOUR ARMOR
Tense and release tension from every part of your body
1. Clench your fists as tight as you can for 5 seconds
2. Release completely. Feel the tension drain away. That's /clear for your muscles!
3. Work up your body: feet → legs → stomach → shoulders → face
4. Finish with 3 deep breaths. Durability fully restored!
🎵
HUMMING — JUKEBOX MODE
Activate your vagus nerve — your body's calm superhighway
1. Take a deep breath in through your nose
2. Hum "hmmmm" slowly as you breathe out — feel the vibration!
3. The vibration activates your vagus nerve — your body's built-in calming system
4. Repeat 5–8 times. Jukebox disc of calm = fully loaded! 🎶

🧠 CBT CRAFTING TABLE — RECRAFT YOUR THOUGHTS

CBT = Cognitive Behavioural Therapy. Turn raw anxious ore into something useful — like a real crafting table, but for your mind!

🪤
HOSTILE THOUGHT MOBS
Identify these mental monsters before they attack!
Creeper: All-or-Nothing — "I failed once so BOOM I'm a total failure"
Witch: Catastrophising — "This will definitely be a complete disaster"
Enderman: Mind Reading — "They're looking at me because they hate me"
Phantom: Fortune Telling — "I just KNOW it's going to go wrong"
Skeleton: Should Statements — "I SHOULD be better at this by now"
Zombie: Personalising — "Everything bad is always my fault"
💬
TALK LIKE YOUR BEST FRIEND
Switch from Hardcore to Survival mode on yourself
1. Write down the harsh thing you're saying to yourself
2. Imagine your best friend is thinking this exact thing about themselves
3. What would you say to THEM? Write that. Now say it to yourself.
Self-compassion builds more resilience than self-criticism. Friendly fire is OFF! 💚

The ACT skill that turns values into real-world change. Tap to expand.
Step 1 — Choose a value: What matters most to you right now?
Step 2 — Choose a direction: If you fully lived this value, what would you be doing differently?
Step 3 — Make a SMART commitment: "This week I will ___ on [day] at [time] for [duration]."
Step 4 — Predict the obstacle: What might get in the way? How will you respond?
Step 5 — Add it as a Quest and check it off when done. XP earned. Legend status growing. ⛏️

STARTING CONVERSATIONS
First contact protocol — easy openers that work IRL
"Hey, what did you think of [class / game / thing they like]?"
"I've been really into [thing lately]. You into anything like that?"
"You seem really good at [thing]. How did you get into it?"
The formula: Ask → Listen → Follow up. Like trading — give and receive! 🤝
🚩
SETTING BOUNDARIES — YOUR CLAIM FLAG
Protect your chunks. You are allowed to say no.
"I can't do that, but I could do ___."
"That doesn't feel right for me." (You don't owe an explanation!)
"I need some time to myself right now."
Boundaries aren't walls. They're claim flags. They protect YOUR territory. 🚩
😤
WHEN YOU'RE ANGRY — DON'T RAGE-QUIT
Express yourself without the regret respawn
Use "I" statements: "I feel hurt when ___ because ___."
Pause before responding — wait until you're below 7/10 anger level
Saying "I need a minute" is always valid. Always.
Ask yourself: will this matter in a year? A month? A week?

Social anxiety makes us freeze. These scripts are ready to use — memorise them like Minecraft crafting recipes!

😰
WHEN YOU'RE NERVOUS AT AN EVENT
Scripts that buy you time and reduce panic
"I'm a bit quiet today — still getting used to being here!"
"I love your [thing]. Where did you get it / how did you find that?"
"I don't always know what to say, but I'm really glad I came."
Remember: everyone is thinking about themselves, not you. You're safer than you feel. 💚
🫂
WHEN YOU NEED SUPPORT
How to ask for help without it feeling impossible
"Hey, I've been having a hard time. Can we talk?"
"I just need someone to listen — you don't have to fix anything."
"I don't fully understand what I'm feeling. Can I just talk through it with you?"
Asking for help is +1 Wisdom. Always. No exceptions. 🧠
⚔️
CONFLICT RESOLUTION — PEACE TREATY
Turn a fight into an understanding
Wait 20 minutes before responding to anything heated.
Start with: "I know we both want this to be okay. Can we talk calmly?"
Use: "When ___ happened, I felt ___ because ___. What I need is ___."
End with: "What do you need from me?" — most arguments end here. 🤝
🔍
READING THE ROOM — BODY LANGUAGE
Decode the signals others are sending
Open: Turned toward you, eye contact, nodding, smiling = engaged
Closed: Arms crossed, looking away, short answers = tired/uncomfortable
Nervous: Touching face, fidgeting, avoiding eye contact = anxious (like you!)
Most people's body language is about THEM, not about you. Don't take it personally.
